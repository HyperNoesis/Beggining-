Desk labelling tool

Pick your desk/room photos (they stay on this device; nothing is uploaded). For each photo tap the desk edge on the LEFT, then on the RIGHT. Flat desk: tap one point and press "Flat". Yellow = your label, cyan = what auto-detect found.



Labels use the same desk line as scenes: y(x)=y+tilt*x (x from 0 to 1 across the picture). Run node tools/e2e/eval_desk.js labels.json <photo folder> for the same report on a computer.
