What you get

Professional rooms

12 ready rooms: newsroom, podcast, classroom, office and more, or use your own picture.

Your name on screen

Lower thirds, tickers, countdowns, scoreboard and QR codes in your brand colours.

Run a whole show

One big NEXT button moves your graphics, scene and teleprompter step by step. Run it from a second phone if you like.

Clean sound

Voice polish, optional background-noise removal, music that lowers when you talk, and sound buttons.

Record or go live

Recording keeps a safety copy as you go. Go live to YouTube or Twitch through a free relay, with a checklist before you start.

Second phone as a camera

Scan a code and a second phone becomes another camera angle.

Honest limits

  • It is a virtual studio, not magic: the background is a picture, so it looks best with the camera held still.
  • Older or low-end phones may need effects turned down. The built-in speed test tells you.
  • A phone browser cannot be a camera for other apps (Zoom, Meet). Use Genesis to record or go live, or use OBS on a computer.
  • Going live to YouTube or Twitch needs a free relay set up once. We walk you through it.
