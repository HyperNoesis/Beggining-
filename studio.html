Skin smoothing

Off by default. Softens skin-coloured areas only; a high setting can look fake. Its cost on a phone is not measured yet: lower it if the video gets choppy.

Auto-framing

Show (run of show)

A list of steps. Tap NEXT (or press Space, the right arrow or Page Down; many presentation clickers send Page Down) and each step can change your lower third, banner, card, ticker, look and audio at once. Back and tapping an earlier step rebuild the on-screen graphics from your steps, so a card you no longer want goes away.

        Using OBS, or a free relay (help)

        Honest limit: a phone browser cannot act as a camera for other apps (no system virtual camera). Two routes work today:

        1. OBS on a computer. Open this studio on the computer. In OBS use Window Capture or Display Capture on the browser window showing the studio preview, then stream from OBS as normal. (Not tested here.)

        2. Free relay. Install MediaMTX (free) on any computer or a free-tier server, use the wizard above to make its config, and paste the WHIP address it prints. It forwards your stream to YouTube or Twitch, which need RTMP.

        Steadier streams: use Wi-Fi, keep the phone plugged in, pick 720p and 1.5 to 2.5 Mbps on a phone, record a copy at the same time. If the picture drops, Genesis lowers quality by itself and restores it later.

        A desktop app that provides a real virtual camera is planned for later; it does not exist yet.
